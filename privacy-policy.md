# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 19, 2026**

COCO ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## What We Collect

- **Account information**: email address and password (stored securely via Supabase)
- **Profile information**: display name, bio, hometown, profile photo
- **Climbing data**: sessions, climbs, grades, notes, locations you enter
- **Approximate location of your sessions**: when you add a gym or crag to a session, COCO saves rough map coordinates for that place, rounded to about 100 metres. If you tap "Use my location", your phone's location is used once to find the place you're at. We only store the rounded coordinates of the session's location — we don't track your location over time or in the background.
- **Usage data**: app activity needed to sync your data across devices

## What We Don't Collect

- We do not sell your data to third parties
- We do not collect data for advertising purposes
- We do not track you across other apps or websites

## How We Use Your Data

- To provide and sync your climbing log across devices
- To power the friends and social features you opt into
- To suggest climbers you might want to follow: people who follow you, people followed by people you follow, and climbers who log sessions at the same gyms or crags as you
- To allow you to delete your account and all associated data at any time
- To review reports, moderate content, and keep the community safe

## Who Can See Your Data

- **Other users** can see your public profile information (name, username, photo, hometown, bio). Your sessions, photos, likes and comments are visible to other users according to your account's privacy settings and who you allow to follow you.
- **COCO administrators**: a small number of authorized members of the COCO team can view all content in the app, including sessions, photos, comments and likes from every account, private accounts included. Administrators use this access only to moderate content, investigate reports, enforce our Terms of Service, provide support, and keep the app safe. This access is never used for advertising and the data is never sold.
- **Suggestions**: if your account is public, you may be suggested to other users who follow the same people you do, or who climb at the same gyms or crags. We show them a short reason, such as "Followed by Alex" or "Climbs at" one of *their own* gyms, but never your session locations or coordinates. Private accounts are never suggested to anyone. Session coordinates are never shown to other users; they're only used by our servers to find matches.
- **Profile QR code**: your profile QR code contains a link with your username. Anyone who scans it can open your profile in COCO, and what they can see follows your privacy settings, the same as finding you in search.

## Reports

When you report a post, comment or account, we store the report along with who filed it. If a comment is reported, we keep a copy of its text, author and time with the report, so administrators can review it even if the comment is later deleted.

## Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase and protected by Row Level Security, so other users can only access what you share with them. Authorized administrators can access all data as described in "Who Can See Your Data" above.

## Third-Party Services

We use a small number of services to run COCO. None of them receive your data for advertising.

- **Supabase** — stores your account and climbing data.
- **Cloudflare** — stores the photos you upload.
- **OpenStreetMap (Nominatim)** — when you search for a place, or when COCO looks up the coordinates of a gym or crag you typed, the place name is sent to OpenStreetMap's search service. Your name, email and account aren't sent with it, though like any web request it can see your device's IP address. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy.
- **Sentry** — receives crash reports and performance data so we can fix bugs. Reports include device and app information, not your name, email or account.
- **Expo** — delivers push notifications to your device.

## Your Rights

- You can edit or delete your profile at any time from the app
- You can delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Delete Account. This includes the approximate coordinates saved for your sessions.
- You can contact us to request a copy of your data
